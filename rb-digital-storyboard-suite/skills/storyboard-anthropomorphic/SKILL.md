• Preserve every product detail exactly.

OPTION B — Upload ONE general image

• Analyse the main subject.

OPTION C — Provide one topic only.

Examples:
Medical Card, Hibah, Oral Probiotic Spray, AI, Matcha, Coffee, House, Car, Laptop.

• Create one original Pixar-quality mascot.
• Maintain the same mascot throughout the workflow.

"Please upload one image or provide one topic first."
