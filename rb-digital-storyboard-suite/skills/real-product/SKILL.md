━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
MULTI-IMAGE LOGIC & DETECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- COMBINED REFERENCE: Treat all uploaded images as cross-references. 
- DUPLICATES: If the same product appears multiple times or from multiple angles, treat it as ONE product. Select the clearest, most front-facing view as the master reference.
- VARIANTS: Treat clearly different packaging, names, sizes, or label colors as separate distinct products.
- OUTPUT COUNT: Generate exactly one separate image for each unique product (e.g., 3 unique products = 3 separate image generation outputs).
- NON-PRODUCT FILTERING: Ignore hands, backgrounds, shelves, cardboard boxes, people, and unbranded accessories. Keep the focus strictly on the main commercial products.
- PARTIAL VIEWS: If a product is partially blocked, use cross-references to reconstruct. If a detail is completely unseen across all images, DO NOT invent it. Prioritize accuracy over completeness.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STRICT PRESERVATION RULES (LOCK)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Treat every product as a fixed physical object. You must preserve its exact real-world identity.

1. GEOMETRY & SHAPE: Keep the exact physical proportions, height, width, silhouette, bottle/box shape, cap, pump, nozzle, and handle. Do not stretch, inflate, or slim the product.
2. LABEL & BRANDING: Preserve the exact logo design, brand name, typography, text hierarchy, label colors, borders, and artwork. 
3. TEXT FIDELITY: Do not rewrite, translate, or guess unclear text. If text is blurry in the reference, preserve its original visual layout. Do not invent fake ingredients or claims.
4. SEPARATION: Never mix parts, labels, caps, or colors between different products. Keep each identity 100% independent.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ENHANCEMENT, LIGHTING & COMPOSITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- ALLOWED ENHANCEMENTS: Upscale resolution, sharpen, reduce digital noise, improve edge clarity, and apply professional studio lighting.
- PROHIBITED ENHANCEMENTS: Over-sharpening, cinematic dark lighting, changing the original material, making it look like CGI, or removing real packaging physical traits.
- COMPOSITION: Square 1:1 format. Product must be upright, centered, and fully visible with comfortable empty margin space. 
- BACKGROUND: Use a clean, plain white or very light neutral studio background. Add only a subtle, realistic contact shadow underneath. No props, no reflections that hide labels, no lifestyle setups.
